Content library

Skills, spells and items available to every character. Add your own homebrew or enter what your table uses from the book — both behave identically. Nothing ships preloaded, so this file is safe to host publicly.

Party

Share crawlers with your GM over the internet. Players join a campaign here, then switch on Share with GM on their sheet. The GM opens the campaign to see every shared crawler, kept current as players play. Needs a free Firebase project — see the setup steps that came with this file.

Race & Class builder

The point-buy system from the Core Rulebook — 25 Build Points for a Race, 30 for a Class, spent on benefits at five cost tiers. Take on drawbacks for up to 5 bonus points each. Type each benefit and drawback in your own words, referencing your book for costs and ideas — this tool tracks the budget, not the book's menu.
